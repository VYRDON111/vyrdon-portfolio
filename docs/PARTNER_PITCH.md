# Partner Overview

## Why Partner With VYRDON

VYRDON's methodology, proof system, and enforcement mechanism are open source. Partnership means:

1. **Research collaboration** — joint papers on formal verification, ZK proofs, or mechanism design
2. **Pilot programs** — test the system against your transaction flows
3. **Integration support** — technical assistance deploying RootPass in your infrastructure
4. **Case studies** — document how the methodology applies to your market

## What We Look For

- Organizations that operate in transaction-heavy environments
- Researchers with expertise in formal methods, ZK, or game theory
- Auditors who can challenge and improve the methodology
- Developers who want to build on the proof system
