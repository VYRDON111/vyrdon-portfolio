# What May Be Wrong

1. The four-pillar model may not cover all transaction verification scenarios
2. The contradiction detection may miss certain types of conflicts
3. The ZK circuit may have under-constrained signals
4. The smart contracts may have undiscovered vulnerabilities
5. The case studies may not generalize to all markets
6. The language may still contain badge-style claims we haven't caught

If you find something wrong, submit it. See `vyrdon-open-review`.
