# Proof Stack

| Level | Method | Tool | What It Proves |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Formal verification | TLA+ | Decision logic is correct for all inputs |
| 2 | Zero-knowledge proof | Circom | Decision is verifiable without revealing evidence |
| 3 | Smart contract enforcement | Solidity | Decision has financial consequences |
| 4 | Evidence ledger | Append-only log | Every decision is recorded immutably |
| 5 | Open review | Public repos | Anyone can challenge the system |
